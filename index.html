1. Overview

Var-Rho EMI Detector v3.1 is a professional tool for electrical workshops and student labs. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app works.

2. Data Collection - NONE

We do NOT collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. The app does NOT require internet permission for detection. All EMI detection happens locally on your device using the smartphone magnetometer sensor.

3. Sensors Used

App uses magnetometer sensor to measure magnetic field variance (Var) and correlation (Rho). This data never leaves your device. It is used only to display Var, Rho, Mag values and graphs.

4. Permissions

• No location permission
• No camera permission
• No microphone permission
• Optional storage permission only if you choose to Export CSV log (user-initiated)

5. Hidden Electronics Finder Disclaimer

Hidden Electronics & Wiring Finder detects magnetic anomalies from ACTIVE electronics and live wires emitting EMI within 5-15cm. It does NOT guarantee detection of OFF, shielded, or non-magnetic devices. It is for assistance and educational purposes only, not a replacement for professional security inspection.
